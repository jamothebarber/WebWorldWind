# Brand Concept — Draft v1 (for approval)

This is a complete draft brand concept for an AI automation + Voice AI agency
that sells and implements on top of GoHighLevel (GHL). Everything here is a
starting point for you to approve, tweak, or replace — nothing is final until
you say so. Anywhere you see a real client name, number, or logo, it's a
clearly-marked placeholder standing in for content you'll supply later.

## Name

**Handled** (wordmark styling: `Handled`, with a small `AI` badge/superscript
in the logo lockup — think "Handled ᴬᴵ"). Used in prose as "Handled" or
"Handled AI" when clarity/SEO calls for it.

Why: it's benefit-first, one word, no jargon — the same instinct behind
"Linear," "Ramp," "Notion." It says the outcome (your calls, leads, and
follow-ups are *handled*) instead of the mechanism (AI, automation, GHL).
That leaves room to talk about voice AI, workflows, CRM, funnels — all of it —
without the name boxing us into "just a voice bot."

Placeholder domain: `gethandled.ai` (or `handled.ai` if available) — swap for
your real domain when decided.

## Positioning

For service businesses (home services, med/dental, legal, real estate,
agencies, anyone who lives or dies by the phone and the follow-up), Handled
builds and runs the AI voice agent + automation system — on GoHighLevel —
that answers every call, qualifies every lead, books the appointment, and
follows up until they show up. Not a chatbot bolted onto a website. Not a
generic "AI agency" that talks in slides. A working system, live in days,
that a business can point at their real phone number and their real pipeline.

**One-liner:** *"Every call answered. Every lead followed up. Automatically."*

**Category we're claiming:** not "AI consulting," not "chatbots" — **AI
front office as a service.**

## Voice & tone

Precision over hype. Short sentences. Concrete claims, not adjectives piled
on adjectives. We show the system working (transcripts, call flows, numbers)
before we tell you it's good. Confident, a little dry, never desperate for
attention — the tone of a team that ships, not a team that pitches.

- Say "answers in under a second," not "blazing fast AI."
- Say "books directly into your calendar," not "seamlessly integrates."
- Humor is allowed in small doses (empty states, 404s), never in the pitch.

## Core copy pillars (what every section should reinforce)

1. **Speed to lead** — most leads go cold in minutes; Handled answers instantly, 24/7.
2. **It's a system, not a bot** — voice AI + CRM + pipelines + follow-up, wired together, not a standalone widget.
3. **Built on what you might already run** — GoHighLevel under the hood, so it fits agencies and businesses already in that ecosystem.
4. **Live fast** — days to launch, not months of "AI transformation."
5. **Proof, not promises** — call transcripts, before/after response-time numbers, real workflows.

## Visual direction

- Dark-first UI (near-black `#0A0A0C` base) with a single confident accent —
  electric indigo/violet (`#6E56F8`-ish) for interactive/AI moments, echoing
  Linear's restraint and ElevenLabs' "AI voice" glow without copying either
  directly.
- One geometric sans for UI/headlines (e.g. a Geist/Inter-class font), one
  monospace for data/transcripts/technical moments (call transcripts, code-like
  workflow snippets).
- Motion language: subtle, physics-based (spring easing), scroll-triggered
  reveals, a waveform/audio-reactive motif reused across hero, voice demo,
  and section dividers to visually tie "voice AI" through the whole page.
